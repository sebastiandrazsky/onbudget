**Complete**
- **Falsy waits (1, 2):** `Retrying._run_wait` in `tenacity/__init__.py` and `AsyncRetrying._run_wait` in `tenacity/asyncio/__init__.py` now sleep `0.0` when `wait` is falsy instead of calling it.

- **`wait_combine` (5):** it now passes the retry state positionally.
- **Release note (6):** `releasenotes/notes/fix-wait-regressions-3f1c9a7e5b2d4c68.yaml` describes the three fixes.
